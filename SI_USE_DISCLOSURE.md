# Super Intelligence (SI) Use Disclosure

**Project:** Eyes on Fires — FIRMS Constellation & Earth Intelligence Explorer  
**Repository:** https://github.com/Just4Skii/firms-constellation-explorer

## Tools used
- ChatGPT (OpenAI)
- DeepSeek V4.1 Flash

## How SI was used
The tools were used as development and research assistants for parts of the application and its supporting research. Use included:
- drafting, refactoring suggestions and debugging assistance for Python and JavaScript code;
- assistance organizing research hypotheses, experiment protocols, validation checks and technical documentation;
- assistance preparing explanatory text and presentation copy.

AI-assisted code was integrated into the project by the developer and checked with project tests and validation scripts. The project also uses explicit data-quality and scientific guardrails; AI assistance does not make a model result a validated forecast.

## Data and imagery
NASA FIRMS, MODIS, VIIRS, LAADS DAAC and NASA Earth imagery are source datasets/products, not AI-generated observations. Other weather, satellite and wildfire records retain their respective source attribution. No synthetic fire observations, detection labels or forecast results are presented as real measurements.

## Images, audio and video
The project demonstration uses an existing application screenshot/detail and programmatically authored interface diagrams. No AI-generated images, video or audio are used in the submitted slide demo.

## Scientific limitations
The current application visualizes satellite detections, weather/environmental layers, a fire-danger index and research evidence. It does not provide a validated operational wildfire-ignition probability or operational wildfire alert.
